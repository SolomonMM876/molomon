How the Google connection works

One-time setup. Each of you shares your Google calendar with the other. In Google Calendar on a computer, open Settings, choose your calendar, and under Share with specific people add your partner with “See all event details”. They accept the emailed link. Then press Connect Google Calendar here, allow read-only access, and tick your calendars and your partner's.

Each visit. Press Connect Google Calendar once. Google remembers your permission, so its window usually closes by itself. Google's sign-in lasts about an hour; press Connect again if the page says it has ended. While connected, both calendars refresh every minute.

Privacy. This page reads Google directly from your browser. Events are not stored and are not sent anywhere else. Names, time zones, hours, and calendar choices are saved on this device only, so each of you sets them on your own device. Events marked Private or Confidential show as “Private event”. Calendars shared as free/busy only show “Busy”. Molomon cannot create or edit Google events.

Disconnect withdraws Molomon's Google permission when you are connected, and forgets the calendar choices on this device. Setup instructions ↗
